01

Executive Summary

InnoBoon Technologies proposes a phased cloud migration and digital transformation engagement for Meridian Financial Services. Our approach leverages proven AWS Landing Zone architecture, zero-downtime deployment pipelines, and AI-assisted monitoring to deliver measurable operational improvements within 20 weeks.

Our lift-shift-optimize model reduces infrastructure costs by an estimated 34% while increasing system availability from 99.7% to 99.99% SLA.

With over 120 successful cloud migrations in the BFSI sector, InnoBoon brings the domain expertise and technical depth Meridian requires at this critical juncture of its digital journey.

02

Technical Approach

Our migration follows a three-phase model: Assessment (Weeks 1–4), Parallel-Run Migration (Weeks 5–14), and Optimisation (Weeks 15–20). Each phase has defined entry/exit criteria and rollback procedures to ensure zero business disruption.

Core banking workloads migrate using blue-green deployment with automated traffic shifting. Data replication leverages AWS Database Migration Service with Change Data Capture to maintain transactional consistency throughout the cutover window.

Security & Compliance

Security is embedded at every layer of the proposed architecture. InnoBoon holds active SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications. All data in transit and at rest is encrypted using AES-256. IAM policies follow least-privilege principles enforced through AWS Organizations SCPs.

Compliance posture aligns with RBI IT Framework, PCI-DSS Level 1, and GDPR requirements as applicable to Meridian's cross-border operations.
