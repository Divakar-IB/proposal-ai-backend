Executive Summary

InnoBoon Technologies proposes a phased cloud migration and digital transformation programme for Meridian Financial Services. The engagement delivers a 34% reduction in infrastructure cost, a 99.99% availability SLA, and a modernised foundation for future AI and data capabilities.

With over 120 successful migrations delivered in the BFSI sector, we bring the domain expertise this programme requires.

Understanding of Requirements

Meridian operates a core banking platform across two data centres with an ageing virtualisation estate.

  • Exit the secondary data centre within the financial year.
  • Achieve a 99.99% availability SLA for tier-1 services.
  • Maintain RBI IT Framework and PCI-DSS compliance throughout.

Proposed Solution

Phased delivery

Delivery follows a three-phase model with defined entry and exit criteria and a documented rollback procedure at every gate.
